Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

In short
  • No accounts. We do not know who you are: no name, email, precise location or contacts.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (for example, which scene was open and for how long). You can turn them off at any time.
  • No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no third-party SDKs.
  • The microphone (Snow scene) and motion sensors work only on your device. Sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • This version is free and has no purchases.

1. Who we are

Colorred ("the app") is developed by Vladyslav Sydorenko ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app treats information on iPhone and Apple Watch. Questions: strongFingers@icloud.com.

2. Data we collect

Anonymous usage statistics, so we know which scenes people enjoy. We use TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service from Germany, as our processor. Events contain:

  • what was used: scene or color mode, time spent, number of remixes, shares and review prompts, night light and breath settings;
  • app version, device model, system version, language and region from your settings;
  • a random ID created when the app is installed and hashed before sending. It is not your Apple ID or advertising ID, and deleting the app resets it.

We do not collect your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos or sound. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app.

To turn it off: on iPhone open Settings, Apps, Colorred and switch off Anonymous statistics; on Apple Watch tap a scene, then ?, and switch off Anonymous statistics on the last page. Each device has its own switch. Statistics not yet sent are deleted.

3. Device features the app uses

FeatureWhyWhat happens to the data
Microphone (Snow scene only, iPhone and Apple Watch)Detects when you blow on the screen so snow can fly.Only the loudness level is read, in real time, on the device. Audio is never recorded, stored or transmitted. The microphone is active only while the Snow scene is on screen. You can deny access; the scene still works without breath.
Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope)Sand, snow and particles follow gravity; shaking remixes a scene.Processed live on the device, never stored or sent.
Photos (iPhone, only when you tap Save or Share)Saves the current gradient as an image.The app adds the image you created. It does not read your library.
Local storageRemembers favorites, color history and settings.Kept on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

4. Purchases

Colorred 3.0 is free and has no in-app purchases.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent any data. Anonymous statistics are processed only by TelemetryDeck on our behalf. Images you share go only where you choose to share them, via the system share sheet.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages. The anonymous statistics do not identify anyone and can be turned off.

7. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar)

You can object at any time with the switch (see section 2). Statistics are not tied to your identity, so we usually cannot find the events of a particular person; write to us and we will help where we can. You can revoke microphone access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone on iPhone, or in the Watch app.

8. Changes

If what we collect changes, this page will be updated before that version ships and the date above will change.
