Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

In short
  • Colorred does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • The microphone (Snow scene) and motion sensors work only on your device. Sound is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card or Apple ID.

1. Who we are

Colorred ("the app") is developed by Vladyslav Sydorenko ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app treats information on iPhone and Apple Watch. Questions: zimm.093@gmail.com.

2. Data we collect

None. The app has no servers of its own and no user accounts. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, identifiers, usage data or diagnostics.

3. Device features the app uses

FeatureWhyWhat happens to the data
Microphone (Snow scene only, iPhone and Apple Watch)Detects when you blow on the screen so snow can fly.Only the loudness level is read, in real time, on the device. Audio is never recorded, stored or transmitted. The microphone is active only while the Snow scene is on screen. You can deny access; the scene still works without breath.
Motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope)Sand, snow and particles follow gravity; shaking remixes a scene.Processed live on the device, never stored or sent.
Photos (iPhone, only when you tap Save or Share)Saves the current gradient as an image.The app adds the image you created. It does not read your library.
Local storageRemembers favorites, color history and settings.Kept on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

4. Purchases

Colorred Pro is a one-time purchase sold through Apple's App Store using In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app only receives a signed confirmation from Apple that the purchase was made, so it can unlock features. We receive aggregated, anonymous sales reports from Apple, with no personal data.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data, because we have none. Images you share go only where you choose to share them, via the system share sheet.

6. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

7. Your rights (GDPR, CCPA and similar)

Because we do not process personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, export or delete on our side. You can revoke microphone access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone on iPhone, or in the Watch app.

8. Changes

If the app ever starts handling personal data, this page will be updated before that version ships and the date above will change.
