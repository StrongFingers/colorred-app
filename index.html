Frequently asked

How do I install Colorred on Apple Watch?

Install Colorred on your iPhone. Open the Watch app > My Watch > scroll to Available Apps > Colorred > Install. Updates arrive on the watch automatically.

The scene disappears and the watch face comes back.

Inside the app tap the scene, then ? for a short guide. In short: on the watch open Settings > Gestures and turn off Wrist Flick; Settings > Display & Brightness > Wake on Wrist Raise on; Settings > General > Return to Clock > Colorred > After 1 hour; Settings > Display & Brightness > Always On > Choose Apps > Colorred on.

The screen goes grey or dim.

That is Always On mode: watchOS dims apps when you lower your wrist. Raise your wrist or tap the screen. If raising the wrist does not wake it, turn on Settings > Display & Brightness > Wake on Wrist Raise. While dimmed, watchOS lets apps draw about one frame per second, so the scene moves in slow motion until the screen wakes.

Blowing on Snow does nothing.

Allow microphone access: on iPhone open Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone > Colorred. Blow from below the watch, like a short exhale. Audio never leaves the device.

Is Colorred free?

Yes. Every scene on iPhone and Apple Watch is free, with no purchases and no ads.

Does Colorred collect my data?

Only anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off in the app. No account, no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
